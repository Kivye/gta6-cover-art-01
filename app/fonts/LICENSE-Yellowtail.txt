Yellowtail — font license attribution

Yellowtail is a typeface designed by Alejandro Paul of the Sudtipos
foundry. It is licensed under the SIL Open Font License, Version 1.1.

This app bundles Yellowtail exactly as Sudtipos issued it, with the
font software left untouched. The full license text is available from
SIL International and from the foundry's own distribution.
